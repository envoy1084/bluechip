# Approvals

## When a trade needs your approval

A trade over an agent's largest trade isn't sent by the agent. It waits for you on the approvals page, with the
trade, the quote and why the agent wants it. An automation set to ask you first waits there too.

## Approving and declining

Approving signs the trade with your passkey (Face ID or your fingerprint), and it is sent from your account and
credited to the agent. Declining ends it, and nothing is sent. When an automation asks you about several trades at
once, one approval lists them all: a single Face ID sends them together in one transaction, and declining declines
them all.

## Approvals expire

An approval left unanswered expires after 6 hours, and nothing is sent. The agent proposes again when it still wants
the trade.
