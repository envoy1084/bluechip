# Approvals

## When a trade needs your approval

A trade over an agent's largest trade isn't sent by the agent. It waits for you, with the trade, the quote and why the
agent wants it. So does a trade from an automation set to Ask me first, or a trade on news that you didn't let go ahead
on its own.

## Where approvals wait

Approvals in the top bar of every page opens what is waiting, with a count, and the Approvals page lists them all. The
An agent needs your approval notification has a Review button that opens them, in the app, by push or in Telegram.

## Approving and declining

Approve with Face ID signs the trade with your passkey, and it is sent from your account and credited to the agent.
Decline ends it, and nothing is sent.

## Several trades at once

When an automation asks you about several trades at once, such as a rebalance, one approval lists them all. Approve 3
trades with Face ID sends them with a single Face ID, and Decline all declines every one.

## Approvals expire

An approval left unanswered expires after 6 hours, and nothing is sent. The card shows how long is left, and the An
approval is about to expire notification comes an hour before. The agent proposes again when it still wants the trade.
