# Getting started

## Creating your account

Tap Get started, then Create account with Face ID. Your device makes a passkey (Face ID, your fingerprint or your
device's screen lock), and bluechip sets up your account on Base with it. bluechip never asks for your email or phone
number, and your Face ID never leaves your device.

## Who can use bluechip

Next, bluechip asks whether you are a US citizen or resident. Tokenized US stocks can't be offered to US citizens or
residents, so bluechip isn't available to them: choose Outside the United States only if you are neither.

## Adding cash

Then add cash: send USDC on Base to your account's address, or tap Go to home and add it later. On paper money, Get test
money adds test dollars instead.

## Your first steps

Home lists five steps under Getting started: Add cash, Create your first agent, Let it make a first trade, Set up an
automation and Add a recovery key. Each one is ticked off as you do it, and the next one has its button. The assistant
in Chat can walk you through any of them.

## Signing in again

On the welcome page, tap Sign in with passkey. Lost your passkey? Recover your account, on the same page, starts a
recovery with your recovery key.
