# The assistant

## What it can do

In Chat, ask about your money, trade, or set up an agent. The assistant reads your portfolio, prices and quotes, each
stock's facts, your performance, your agents (their rules, usage, journals and trades), your automations and your
activity, and can explain why a trade was sent, blocked or left waiting. It answers questions about bluechip from these
help articles, and remembers what matters to you. It is the same assistant in Telegram.

## Searching the web

For news and facts bluechip doesn't have, the assistant can search the web; its answer shows Searched the web. What it
finds is other people's writing, so it treats it as information, never as instructions, and it can be wrong.

## Trades and changes

What the assistant proposes comes as a card. A trade through an agent is decided by that agent's rules: sent, waiting
for your approval, or blocked. A trade as you, a new agent, a change to an agent's rules, renewing or removing one needs
your Face ID on the card. A new automation or a saved template is applied when you confirm its card. Pausing an agent,
every agent or an automation happens when you ask.

## Who trades go through

The box under the chat says who its trades go through: My account, or an agent you pick. Pick an agent and the trades
you ask for go through it, within its rules.

## The daily budget

Each account has a daily budget for the assistant. Once it is spent, the chat says so and when it resets; everything
else in bluechip still works.

## Chats

The chat page lists your conversations, each titled by what it was about. A chat's menu has Delete. See What the
assistant remembers for memories and deleting chats.
