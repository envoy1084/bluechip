# Trading

## What you hold

The stocks on bluechip are Coinbase's tokenized stocks on Base. Each is priced by Chainlink's NAV feed for it, and
traded in its USDC pool on Aerodrome. Your cash is USDC.

## Trading yourself

You can buy and sell from your own account at any time; each trade is signed with your passkey. The quote shows what
you get at least; a trade that would fill too far from it fails, and nothing is traded.

## Prices on weekends and holidays

When markets are closed, Chainlink's prices are paused. Agents whose fair price policy doesn't trade on paused prices
wait until they move again.
