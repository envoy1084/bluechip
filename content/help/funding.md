# Adding and withdrawing cash

## Adding cash

Send USDC on Base to your bluechip account's address. It usually shows up in about a minute. Only send USDC on Base;
other tokens or networks don't reach your account as cash.

## Withdrawing

A withdrawal sends USDC from your account to an address you give, on Base. It is signed with your passkey, and you can
cancel it before you sign.
