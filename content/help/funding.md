# Adding and withdrawing cash

## Adding cash

Choose Add cash to see your account's address and its QR code. Send USDC on Base to that address, or scan the code with
your wallet: it opens a USDC transfer on Base with your address filled in. Your address is also in Settings, under
Account. Only send USDC on Base; other tokens or networks don't reach your account as cash.

## When cash arrives

Cash shows in your balance once Base has it, usually within seconds. With the Add cash dialog open you see it arrive,
and the Cash arrived notification tells you too.

## Withdrawing

Choose Withdraw on the Portfolio page. Enter the amount and an address on Base, then Review withdrawal, and sign it with
Face ID. The USDC goes from your account to that address. Only send to an address that accepts USDC on Base: sends can't
be undone. Until you sign, Change amount or address takes you back and nothing moves.

## After a withdrawal

The withdrawal shows on the Portfolio page with a link to BaseScan, and the Withdrawals notification tells you when it
lands or fails. A failed withdrawal leaves your cash where it was.
