# Portfolio and activity

## Your portfolio

The Portfolio page shows what you hold, its value over time, your mix and your cash, with Add cash and Withdraw. Profit
in all is split into Realised, from what you sold, and Unrealised, on what you still hold. By stock and agent shows
where it came from.

## How profit is counted

Every fill is counted at what it really cost: the pool's fee, slippage against Chainlink's price and gas. Each stock's
cost is counted first in, first out. bluechip checks your balances on Base every few minutes; cash that arrives is a
deposit, and anything no trade, deposit or withdrawal explains is flagged with the Money moved outside bluechip
notification.

## Activity

Activity lists every trade, deposit and withdrawal, yours, your agents' and your automations', filtered by type and
period. Each entry shows what you paid and got, the fair price check and a link to BaseScan.

## Audit log

In Settings, Audit log lists everything that happened to your account: passkeys, devices, agents, automations,
recoveries and more, by you, an agent, the assistant, an automation or bluechip. Search it and download it.
