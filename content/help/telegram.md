# Telegram

## Linking Telegram

In Settings, under Notifications, choose Link Telegram, then Open Telegram: bluechip's bot opens, tap Start, and the
chat links itself. The link works once, for 10 minutes. With Telegram on another device, send the bot the /start message
shown, with its code, in a private chat. You can link more than one chat.

## What you can do there

Talk to the same assistant as in the app, and get the notifications you send to Telegram. Commands: /portfolio (your
cash, stocks and total value), /agents, /pause (one agent), /pause_all, /cancel_recovery, /unlink and /help. Only the
person who linked a chat can use it.

## What needs the app

Nothing is signed in Telegram. Changes the assistant sets up there are confirmed with buttons; a trade or a change that
needs your Face ID comes as a link into the app. Pausing agents and cancelling a recovery work right in the chat,
without Face ID.

## Unlinking

Choose Unlink next to the chat in Settings, or send /unlink in the chat. Linking and unlinking a chat are security
notifications, so you always hear about them.
