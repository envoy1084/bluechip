# Notifications

## Where notifications go

Every notification lands In app, in the bell in the top bar. Each kind can also go by Push, to every device you turned
push on, and to Telegram once you link it. Choose which in Settings, under Notifications: a switch per kind and channel.

## What you get by default

- Everywhere: An agent needs your approval, An agent's session is ending, Tell me, and Money moved outside bluechip.
- In app and Push: An approval is about to expire.
- In app and Telegram: Trades done, A policy stopped a trade, An agent was paused, An automation ran, A judgment held an
  automation back, Cash arrived and Withdrawals.
- In app only: A trade waits too long and An agent was renewed.

## Security notifications

A new passkey, a passkey removed, a new device signed in, your recovery key changed, a recovery asked for, cancelled or
completed, and a chat linked or unlinked always reach you on every channel, through quiet hours. They can't be turned
off. Someone asked to recover your account has a Cancel recovery button.

## The morning digest

An automation ran and A judgment held an automation back go Only in the morning digest to start: one message each
morning at 08:00 in your time zone, In app and in Telegram, with everything held for it. You can also send Trades done,
A trade waits too long and An agent was renewed only in the digest.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours hold Push and Telegram until they end; In app still shows everything. A window past midnight carries on into
the next day. Security notifications never wait.

## Push notifications

Push is turned on per device, under Push notifications: Push on this device. On iPhone, add bluechip to your Home Screen
first. If your browser blocks notifications for bluechip, allow them in its site settings.
