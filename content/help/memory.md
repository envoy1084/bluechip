# What the assistant remembers

## Memories

The assistant keeps what you tell it to remember, and notes preferences, limits, goals and facts from what you say.
What it only guesses waits for you to confirm before it is used; a guess you never confirm is dropped after 30 days.

## Earlier conversations and journals

It also recalls what was said in earlier conversations, summaries of long ones, and your agents' journals: what each
agent did and why, written by bluechip as it happened.

## Seeing, exporting and deleting

The memory page lists every memory, where it came from and how many answers used it. You can search them, download
everything as JSON, and delete one or all. Deleting a memory also stops the assistant recalling the conversation turn
it came from; deleting everything also forgets the summaries and what it could recall from earlier conversations.
Your conversations themselves stay in your history.
