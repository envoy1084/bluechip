# What the assistant remembers

## Memories

The assistant keeps what you tell it to remember, and notes preferences, limits, goals and facts from what you say.
What it only guesses waits for you to confirm before it is used; a guess you never confirm is dropped after 30 days.

## Earlier conversations and journals

It also recalls what was said in earlier conversations, summaries of long ones, and your agents' journals: what each
agent did and why, written by bluechip as it happened.

## Seeing, exporting and deleting

The memory page lists every memory, where it came from and how many answers used it. You can search them, download
everything as JSON, and delete one or all. Deleting a memory also deletes the conversation turn it came from: your
message and the assistant's answer leave the conversation, the assistant can no longer recall them, and a summary of
that conversation that included them is written again without them. Deleting everything forgets every memory, the
summaries and what it could recall from earlier conversations, while your conversations stay in your history.

## Deleting a chat

Each chat in the chat page's list has a menu with Delete. Deleting a chat deletes its messages, its cards and its
summary, and the assistant can no longer recall what was said in it; an answer still being written stops first. What
the assistant remembers from it stays on the memory page, to keep or delete there, and trades it made stay in your
activity.
