# Paper money

## What paper money is

Paper money is bluechip on Base Sepolia, Base's test network, with test dollars. Everything works as it does with
real money: your passkey, agents with limits locked onchain, automations, the assistant and Telegram, at live market
prices. The dollars and shares are test tokens worth nothing, so you can try bluechip without risking anything. A
"Paper money" badge shows on every page.

## Getting test money

Choose Get test money in Add cash, while signing up or in Settings. Each tap adds $1,000 of test USDC to your account,
up to $10,000 a day; the allowance starts again at midnight UTC, and the button shows what is left today. You can also
send test USDC on Base Sepolia to your account's address.

## What is the same

Your account, passkeys and recovery keys work as they do on Base. Trades are checked against a reference price, and
agents can never go past the policies you lock with Face ID. Prices follow the stock market: they move while US
markets trade, around the clock on weekdays, and pause on weekends and market holidays. bluechip pays the network fee
for every trade.

## What is different

The stocks are test tokens standing in for Coinbase's tokenized stocks, and they trade on bluechip's paper exchange
rather than Aerodrome, at the market price plus the pool's fee and a small price impact for larger trades. Prices come
from bluechip's own feeds, written from live market prices, rather than Chainlink's. Nothing on paper money can be
moved to Base, and links open on Base Sepolia's explorer.
