# Analytics and privacy

## What bluechip measures

On paper money and with real money, bluechip sends PostHog product analytics: the pages you open, key steps such as
signing up, adding cash, making an agent or trading, and counts such as how many agents you have, under your bluechip
user id. Never your name, address, balances or anything you write. Some sessions are recorded as replays, which never
show what you type; with real money they hide all text on the screen. For the assistant, PostHog gets the model, the
tokens and the cost of each answer, never the words.

## Turning product analytics off

In Settings, switch off Product analytics. bluechip then sends no analytics events or replays about you. A browser that
asks sites not to track it sends no analytics from the app either.

## What keeps running

bluechip's own telemetry, the traces, logs and metrics that keep it working, still runs: it carries ids, never your
messages, memories or keys, and is deleted after 30 days.

## Your data

You can download your audit log, export and delete your memories, delete chats, unlink Telegram and sign out devices. To
close your account and have bluechip delete what it holds, contact bluechip. What is on Base is public and stays there.
The privacy policy has the details.
